### What these changes do

1. **Wind convention fixed** — `lift_line_direction()` now returns downwind (+X), not upwind (-X). The lift kite flies DOWNWIND of the hub. Comment and sign corrected in both `lift_kite.jl` and `ring_forces.jl`.

2. **Backline becomes quasi-static catenary** — instead of a simple tension-only spring with a fixed L₀, the backline now uses a proper catenary model (`src/catenary.jl`). The anchor is at `back_anchor_fwd_x` downwind of the hub's x-projection. Design rest length = straight-line distance from anchor to design hub position.

3. **Furl is now geometric (payout-based)** — the `backline_payout` field on SystemParams permits winch release. When payout > 0, L₀ increases, line goes slack, hub rises under lift → β increases → power spills. No more stiffness-killing hack. The furl scenario in visualization.jl was updated accordingly (constant wind, geometric spill).

4. **Wind indicator** — changed from a static orange arrow to animated "marching ants" blue-grey dots streaming upwind of the rotor with fade-out.
