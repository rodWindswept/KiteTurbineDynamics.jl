## The Furl Scenario Bug
**Symptom:** The furl scenario pays out the backline, but the lift bearing no longer rises to spill the wind.
**Root Cause:** The furl mechanism previously relied on the lift force pointing upwards at 80°. When the backline was released, this steep vertical pull lifted the entire rotor. Because we placed `lifter_anchor` on the 30° shaft axis to fix the bridle-length geometry constraint, the lift force now pulls at 30° (along the shaft) instead of 80° (upwards). When the backline pays out, it goes slack, but there is no upward aerodynamic force left to lift the bearing.
