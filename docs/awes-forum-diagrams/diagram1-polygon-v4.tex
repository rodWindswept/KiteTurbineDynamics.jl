  substantial blade and hardware mass.\\[3pt]
  \textbf{3. Corrected physics:} The $\tan{\rightarrow}\sin$ polygon force correction and
  $\cos^3{\rightarrow}\cos^{2.65}$ elevation correction eliminated artificial safety margins that
  had hidden the true structural cost.};
