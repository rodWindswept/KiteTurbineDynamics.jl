## 2. Why not four panels

The text block is 174 mm, 6.850 in.

A four-up plate gives each panel (174 − 3) / 2 = **85.5 mm, 3.366 in** of slot.

The figures seat pinned the legibility floor at `ddc9d67`.

At that floor 7.5 pt on a 7.6 in canvas prints at 6.76 pt in this block.
The 7 pt rows print at 6.31 pt.

Any slot under 6.51 in drops the rows below 6 pt, and that is a plate re-cut.

A 3.366 in slot scales the canvas by 0.443.

So 7.5 pt type prints at **3.32 pt**, and the 7 pt rows at **3.10 pt**.

That is half the floor.

The floor needs 6.08 in per panel for 7.5 pt type, and 6.51 in for 7 pt.

Four panels at the tighter figure need **24.3 in, 618 mm**, of width.

The block is 174 mm.

The four-up misses by a factor of 3.5, and no A4 layout closes that gap.

Two panels stacked keep the full 6.85 in slot.

Type then prints at 6.76 pt and 6.31 pt, which is the `eval-timeline` case at `ddc9d67`.
