Status key.

- **R1** — spec and mock open.
- **R2** — the scripted figure generated. The checks run.
- **planned** — a spec or seed exists.
- **proposed** — this map is the seed.

## 3. The unblock chain

- **The analysis room:** the pair extract, the winner operating point,
  the arrays in the pair SPECs. The strip needs the best-per-generation
  genomes and the selection rule. The hero needs the winner genome. The
  family grid needs the exemplars. The renderer draws what it receives.
  It selects nothing.

- **The validators:** register rows for the twist mechanism values and
  for any printed protocol value. The swept area value joins if an
  annotation carries one. The fidelity band joins too (row 12 merged into row 4, v46). No number prints
  without its row.

- **Track D:** the display forms and the anatomy terms.

- **Track B:** the failure entries, each with its lesson.

- **@author:** the pair slotting beside the census and the envelope. The
  captions. The annotation wording.

- **@figures-images-diagrams:** the capture scripts (plan, side,
  downwind, from the built state). The pair generation runs. F-VALUE sits
  at the sign-off commit. The strip and family sets when the extracts
  land. The timeline when Track B reconciles.
