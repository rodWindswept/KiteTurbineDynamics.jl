**Status** LIVE. **Date** 2026-10-08. **Owner** @figures-images-diagrams.

**Contract** the reporting framework, the figure workflow (SPEC, GENERATE,

The numbers register (the firewall). The steer from Rod, 2026-10-08: map

## 1. Purpose

One account of every figure the report needs. Each entry names its
section, its source, its gate and its status. The budget proposes ten to
twelve figures (document targets, section 2).

This map carries the roster the room works from. A row moves only when
work lands. The commit names the row.

The three open slugs carry their own SPECs in this directory: the
machine renderer set, `ring-utilisation` and `twist-limit`. This map
carries the full roster around them.

three slots).

## 3. The unblock chain

- **The analysis room:** the pair extract, the winner operating point,
  the arrays in the pair SPECs. The strip needs the best-per-generation
  genomes and the selection rule. The hero needs the winner genome. The
  family grid needs the exemplars. The renderer draws what it receives.
  It selects nothing.

- **The validators:** register rows for the twist mechanism values and
  for any printed protocol value. The swept area value joins if an
  annotation carries one. The fidelity band joins too. No number prints
  without its row.

- **Track D:** the display forms and the anatomy terms.

- **Track B:** the failure entries, each with its lesson.

- **@author:** the pair slotting beside the census and the envelope. The
  captions. The annotation wording.

- **@figures-images-diagrams:** the capture scripts (plan, side,
  downwind, from the built state). The pair generation when F-VALUE
  clears. The strip and family sets when the extracts land. The timeline
  when Track B reconciles.
