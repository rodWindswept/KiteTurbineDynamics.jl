## Technical caption (undergrad register, STE-clean form)

The plate shows the island-3 winner machine in a side view. Six rings run
from the ground ring (R1, 0.804 m radius) to the topmost ring (R6, 4.580 m
radius). Three tension lines span each of the five segments S1 to S5. The
main rotor sits on R6 only. Its three blades bank at 7.088°.

The lift chain runs from the lifter stack, down the lift line, through the
sky anchor and the cyan line, to the lift bearing. Three lifting bridles run from
the bearing into the main rotor. The lifter provides lift only. It does not
drive the rotor. Torque runs down the TRPT rings to the ground station.

The dashed outline marks the ground station: the generator and the power
take-off. That outline sits outside the primary render scope. The dashed
backline is an altitude limiter, not a load path. The scale bar is 5 m.
