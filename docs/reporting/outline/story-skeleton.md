# Story skeleton: one sentence per chapter

**Slug** `story-skeleton`

**Status** PREP v1. Chapter order follows the reporting framework, the
content units U1 to U6. One sentence carries the claim of each chapter.

Numbers-free by construction. The sentences are candidate claims until
the Track A and Track B responses from the analysis room land in
`docs/reporting/room-responses/`.

**Owner** @author. **Gate** room reconciliation when Track A and Track B
land, then @science-validator on facts, and Rod on voice.

**Contract** the reporting framework (content units, scientific
structure) and the room consultation (Tracks A and B). Companion:
`docs/reporting/outline/awe-context-outline.md` supplies the U1 context.

## The story in one line

A computational evolution searched a kite-turbine design space. This
report shows the machine it found, the levers that shaped it, and the
failures that taught the most.

## Chapter claims

**U1, Introduction.** The report places the kite turbine in the airborne
wind energy landscape: ground generation, rotary transmission, tensile
structure.

- Feeds from: the AWE-context outline.
- Reconcile with: Track A.
- Assembly: Introduction.

**U2, The levers.** The levers chapter names what moves the machine and
ranks each design choice by its effect on power, mass and survival.

- Feeds from: the landscape analysis (lever ranking and interactions).
- Reconcile with: Track A, and the lever questions in the consultation.
- Assembly: Results.

**U3, The evolution story.** The evolution chapter renders the search
generation by generation, as the turbines themselves, at one shared
scale.

- Feeds from: the search record and the machine renderer.
- Reconcile with: Track A and Track B (what the journey shows).
- Assembly: Results.

**U4, Design implications.** The design implications chapter states what
each design choice costs and buys across mass, force, scaling, torque and
power.

- Feeds from: the regime table and the scaling laws.
- Reconcile with: Track A.
- Assembly: Results.

**U5, Achievements.** The conclusion states what the work achieved,
anchored on the measured Daisy machine and on results that survived
honest windows.

- Feeds from: DECISIONS, the trust ledger, signed register rows.
- Reconcile with: Track B (story elements and their lessons).
- Assembly: Conclusion.

**U6, The failure ledger.** The failure ledger tells what did not work
and states the lesson each failure carries.

- Feeds from: the trust ledger and the archive records.
- Reconcile with: Track B (each failure enters with its lesson).
- Assembly: Discussion.

## Reconciliation checklist for the room responses

1. **Structure.** Compare chapter order and count with the outline
   proposed by Track A. Adopt the changes from Track A where they improve
   the path for the reader.

2. **Claims.** Adopt the claims from Track A where they beat these
   drafts. Keep one sentence per chapter, and keep them numbers-free.

3. **Story elements.** Audit against Track B: the Daisy anchor, honest
   windows, tension-supplied capacity, the fold, the k-alignment faults,
   the dead geometry genes, the bank derate, and the Phase 0 signing.
   Keep an element only with its lesson and its mission link (valid
   science, development speed, or the clean-energy case).

4. **Numbers rule.** No sentence here reveals a number. Values attach
   only at chapter production, from signed register rows, by row ID.

5. **Re-gate.** Science-validator on facts, Rod on voice, then assembly.
