{\Large\sffamily\bfseries TRPT Design Landscape}

\vspace{4pt}

{\footnotesize\sffamily\color{textDim} Windswept \& Interesting Ltd · July 2026 · V10 Tight · 11 m/s · DE-optimized ring geometry}

\vspace{10pt}

{\small
\textbf{Background.} The TRPT (Tensioned Rotary Power Transmission) kite turbine uses a rotating ring of blades suspended on tensioned tethers to extract wind power at altitude. This report maps the design envelope for the V10 Tight configuration — 13 viable designs discovered in a 50-point catalog sweep across blade scale ($\lambda$\,0.69--1.10), generator load ($k_{\text{mppt}}$\,2--14), tether diameter (3.5--4\,mm), and ring radius ($r_{\text{bottom}}$\,1.00--1.30). All simulations use the physically-correct per-vertex Dyneema spoke spring model (tension-only, replacing the earlier centre-constraint model which inflated FoS values), DE-optimized ring tube geometry ($D_o{=}60$\,mm, $t/D{=}0.01$), and 30-second MPPT sustain at 11\,m/s wind speed. Designs pass the dual gate of $P \ge 50$\,kW ground power and ring Factor of Safety $\text{FoS} \ge 1.5$ against Euler buckling.

\textbf{How to read the table.} $\lambda$ (blade scale factor) is swept area relative to ring diameter — larger blades produce more power but increase ring compression. $k_{\text{mppt}}$ is the generator load control parameter ($P_{\text{gen}} = k \cdot \omega^3$) — higher $k$ extracts more torque per rpm but lowers FoS. FoS is the ring's buckling safety margin: $\ge 6$ is ultra-safe, $4$--$6$ is production-ready, $2.5$--$4$ is adequate with monitoring, $<2.5$ is marginal (engineering margin only, needs structural reinforcement for field deployment). Variant superscripts: $^{3.5}$ = 3.5\,mm tether, $^{\text{r}}$ = tight ring ($r_{\text{bottom}}{=}1.00$); unmarked = standard 4\,mm tether, $r_{\text{bottom}}{=}1.30$.

\textbf{Key findings.} Two operating regimes exist: Regime A (high-torque, 168--408\,rpm, blades $\ge 0.90$) and Regime B (high-RPM, 200--482\,rpm, blades $\le 0.85$). Regime B is partially hidden from the catalog sweep by a settle-to-operational bug ($\omega$ ceiling of 91\,rpm blinds the optimizer to 300--480\,rpm equilibria). Kickstart tests recovered blade 0.85 at $k{=}2$ producing 116--156\,kW at 326--482\,rpm. The Pareto front runs from blade 0.90·k6 (81\,kW, FoS 11.3, safest) through 0.95·k4 (199\,kW, FoS 5.2, sweet spot) to 1.10·k4 (297\,kW, FoS 2.3, max power). See the companion scatter plot for the full P--$\omega$ landscape.
}
