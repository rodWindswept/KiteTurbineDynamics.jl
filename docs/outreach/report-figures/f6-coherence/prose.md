# F6 — prose: "The two campaigns" (corrected era vs the voided attempt)

Every campaign leaves a ghost behind. The first 5 kW attempt ran on the
evening of 14 August 2026, on an evaluator with a hole in it: a design
whose hub ring diverged — angular velocity racing toward 10^66 rad/s —
could still pass the ground-side checks. The fix landed ninety minutes
later, and the first attempt's results were voided and re-run. This figure
puts the two attempts side by side: the voided trace dashed, the corrected
campaigns solid, same seeds, same lengths, same 30 generations.

The eye should land on how similar the two searches look. At 18 m both
descend to nearly the same plateau; at 21.2 m the shapes track each other
almost line for line. At 25 m the corrected search goes deeper — −7.31
against the voided attempt's −6.88 — but at 18 m the voided attempt's
island 1 reached −6.66, deeper than anything the corrected campaign found.
The old metric was more generous. That is the point.

The two traces are not the same measurement. The pre-fix evaluator scored
without the hardening gates that now stand between a design and the
report: hub sanity, tip-speed ceiling, cp falloff, per-rotor Betz, and the
SK99 rope-break strain. A design the old metric rewarded could be one the
corrected model disqualifies — and the discipline that catches it is the
re-gate, not the search. The voided attempt's own best design still passes
today's gate (6.3 kW, no break, 5.7 m clearance) — a sound machine from a
suspect era, kept as evidence, never cited as a result.

The dashed lines are the report's honesty: what we almost published, and
why we didn't.
