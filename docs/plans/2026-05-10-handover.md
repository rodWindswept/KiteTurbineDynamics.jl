# Handover — 2026-05-10 to 2026-05-11

## What we were trying to do
Implement disc tilt so the rotor/TRPT rings pitch during furl, spilling power
through changed tether geometry. Started with Option B (quasi-static tilt from
bridle torque), iterated through several approaches.

## What Rod observed in the dashboard (these are the real problems)

1. **Bridle lines uneven** — the 5 gold lines from bearing to hub ring look
   different lengths. This persisted through all changes. Rod described it as
   "terribly uneven resulting in shaking at the top in rotation." Each time
   this problem appears, the TRPT ring utilisation colours also look wrong.

2. **Frame 1 jump toward generator** — frame 0 (initialised state) looks fine,
   symmetric, on-axis. Frame 1 snaps hard toward the ground anchor. Adding a
   wind-aware pre-settle (2000 steps with actual wind+lift before the main
   simulation loop) did not fix this.

3. **Bearing shoots off during furl** — at ~4 seconds into a 20-second furl
   scenario, the bearing launched downwind and downward. Temporarily mitigated
   by increasing bearing mass and damping, but root cause not understood.

4. **Lift HUD showed 0 N** — fixed (was reading stale SimFrame captured with
   `lift_device=nothing`; now computes T_lift live), but Rod didn't get to
   verify the fix because of issues 1-3.

5. **At one point** (~commit `ea088c3`) Rod said "Ahhhh great, looking good" —
   the initial geometry was clean. But furl behaviour was broken. Everything
   since then has been attempts to fix furl without breaking the clean init.

## Key facts established today

- **Bridle L0 are mathematically equal**: all = sqrt(36 + hub_R²) at construction.
  Verified analytically and with Julia. For 50kW: L0 = 7.4833 m for all 5 lines.
- **Dashboard now matches physics**: `_perp_fn` uses `shaft_perp_basis(normalize(hub_pos))`,
  same as `rope_forces.jl`. Previously used tilted basis — caused visual mismatch.
- **Tilt feedback was unstable**: torque→tilt→geometry→torque loop blew up in 77 steps.
  Fixed by making tilt one-way (bearing offset → tilt angle, no feedback through bridles).
- **376 tests pass** throughout all changes.

## Where to look tomorrow

The bridle unevenness Rod sees is the central mystery. Possibilities:

1. **Construction bug** — something in `_build_kite_turbine_system_impl` lines 144-153
   produces different L0 for different lines. Check: `src/initialization.jl:144-153`
2. **Settling introduces asymmetry** — `settle_to_equilibrium` distorts the bearing
   position. Check: `src/initialization.jl:269-316`
3. **`settle_to_operational_state` corrupts state** — the twist bisection in
   `src/initialization.jl:491-583` might shift ring positions.
4. **Dashboard rendering bug** — despite matching physics basis, something in the
   `@lift` closures produces different positions. Check: `src/visualization.jl:350-362`
5. **Rod is seeing current lengths not rest lengths** — the L0 are equal but the
   bearing has drifted, making current Euclidean distances differ. Check bearing
