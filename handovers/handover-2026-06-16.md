## What this session accomplished

### Three physics corrections applied to `src/`

1. **Polygon force resolution: tan → sin**
   - `trpt_optimization.jl:451` — beam compression formula
   - `expansion_rotor.jl:172` — geometry factor
   - `trpt_axial_profiles.jl:25,34` — comments
   - `expansion_rotor.jl:196` — docstring
   - Error: tan(π/n) understates compression by cos(π/n), factor of 2 at n=3

2. **Elevation power exponent: cos³ → cos²·⁶⁵**
   - `sim_frame.jl:158` — frame capture power calculation
   - `initialization.jl:723` — initial rotor speed equilibrium
   - These two files missed the June 10 BEM fix that updated `ring_forces.jl`

3. **Coupled knuckle mass model**
   - New function `knuckle_mass_at_ring(Do, t_over_D, n_sides)` in `trpt_optimization.jl`
   - Mass derived from beam geometry: m = ρ·π·Do·t·(2·L_clamp + L_bend)
   - `L_clamp = KNUCKLE_L_CLAMP_FACTOR · Do` (default 1.0, weight-optimised cuff)
   - `L_bend = Do·4/n_sides` (arc through polygon angle)
   - Removed `knuckle_mass_kg` from `TRPTDesignV4` struct and design vector
   - `TRPT_V4_DIM`: 10→9, `TRPT_V6_DIM`: 12→11
