## Caveats and open questions

1. **Hardware ω ceiling?** Rod to specify: generator rpm limit, tether wrap-rate
   limit, tip-speed noise constraint. The Mach-0.85 limit is model-driven; a
   hardware limit may be tighter.

2. **R1 spike investigation.** The t=57-59s oscillation at k=6.23 for V10 Tight
   @ 11 m/s may be dynamic instability. Needs extended trace with ω(t) and FoS(t)
   to diagnose. If confirmed, low k is dynamically unstable for Tight, and the
   constrained optimum is at the stability boundary.

3. **λ=0.69 peak flatness.** R3's peak at k=3.0 has flatness 1.008 — the design
   is robust to k choice. This may be the best path for a production design.

4. **Compressibility correction?** Expansion rotors at Mach 0.7-0.9 see drag
   divergence. Subsonic BEM polars are optimistic. A Prandtl-Glauert or similar
   correction may be needed for accurate power at high ω. Flagged as caveat for now.
