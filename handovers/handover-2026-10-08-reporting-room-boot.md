  specs, story skeletons, character settling) needs no numbers — do that now.

  introduced with a definition at first use. Shortcut jargon is a release
  blocker.

  not in the register, request it — do not derive it.
- **Figure workflow:** spec (claim + register rows + axes + dual-caption
  plan) → generate (script + source stamp) → external check (validators on

  Rod on the final visual pass). Three rounds, spec evolves by deltas.

  stripped, numbers exact.
