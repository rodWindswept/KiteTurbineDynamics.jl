# Handover Report - 12 May 2026

## Executive Summary
We have transitioned the lift system to a **Dynamic Sky Anchor** model. The 80° kite lift and ground backline now attach to a free particle (the Sky Anchor) which is connected to the TRPT bearing via a 5m "Cyan Line". This architecture correctly supports the "Furl" scenario (lifting the rotor to spill wind) in principle, but the initialization physics are currently fighting the ODE solver.

## Key Changes
1.  **Physics Model:** Moved `T_lift` and Backline catenary forces from the `BearingNode` to the `SkyAnchorNode`.
2.  **Parameters:** Updated `back_anchor_fwd_x` to 11.0m (downwind) to allow a stable tension balance on the 30° shaft axis.
3.  **Furl Control:** Removed the problematic "pitch boost" from the furl sequence. It was generating ~16kN of horizontal drag, turning the rotary lifter into a parachute that yanked the system downwind. Furl now relies purely on backline payout.
4.  **Initialisation:** Added an axial contraction (shrinkage) step to the torque-chain bisection to account for the physical shortening of the TRPT under twist.

## Findings from Grill Session
1.  **Force Imbalance:** The "violent shifts" in the UI are caused by a massive acceleration spike at Frame 1. The Sky Anchor and Bearing are not perfectly balanced at Frame 0.
2.  **The "Slack Line" Initialization & Preload Deletion:** We discovered that the real culprit behind the massive force imbalance (and the TRPT lines appearing slack/grey at initialization) is the new "zero strain" twist contraction logic. By geometrically contracting the segments to their exact rest length during the twist bisection, we inadvertently deleted the axial preload. Without axial preload, the TRPT has no torsional rigidity at start-up, causing the hub to spin wildly and the system to violently "snap" taut once the simulation begins. (Note: We briefly investigated wind shear between 15m and 20m altitude, but the 1/7th power law only accounts for a few percent difference in dynamic pressure—nowhere near enough to explain the catastrophic failure).

## Next Steps for the Next Agent
- **Tensioned Bisection:** Fix the bisection loop in `initialization.jl` to solve for the **design axial preload**, not zero-tension geometry. This will ensure the TRPT starts stiff and "coral" (taut) without needing a complex ground-launch sequence.
- **Furl Evaluation Metric:** When testing if Furl works, do not just check if the Z altitude rises. Check the **elevation angle** of the TRPT rotor/shaft. The goal of furl is to pitch the rotor up to spill wind. 
- **Settling Robustness:** Increase the number of steps or reduce damping in the operational settle loop so the Sky Anchor can naturally find its 3-way force balance before the simulation starts.
- **Clean Up:** Remove the temporary `test_*.jl` files and the malformed `src/visualization.jl.patch` if still present.
