SLIDER vs. OPTION — DASHBOARD k_mppt WIDGET CRITICAL DISCOVERY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROBLEM: Dashboard slider (1–50 integer range) clips k_mppt for 50 kW designs.

  k_mppt_50kw = 11.0 × (50/10)^2.5 = 614.9 N·m·s²/rad²
  Slider default: clamp(614.9, 1, 50) = 50   ← 12.3× WRONG

  At k_mppt=615, MPPT torque at 41 rpm (rated ω):
    τ = 615 × (4.29)² = 11,530 Nm

  V10 rotor static torque: ~237 Nm  ← cannot overcome MPPT
  Result: V10 winner stalls at near-zero rpm in dashboard (pitfall #20)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STABILITY BOUNDARY — k×4.0 CATASTROPHIC REGIME
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Metric              k×1.5 (optimal)     k×4.0 (FAILED)
  ──────              ───────────────     ──────────────
  Twist σ (ripple)    ~0°                 50–80°
  Δω hub−ground       ~0.003 rad/s        large slip events
  Time-series         smooth settle       periodic snapping/collapse
  Power               stable              drops to zero intermittently
  Tether load         nominal             violent spiking

  Root cause: MPPT torque overpowers the torsional stiffness of the TRPT
  shaft.  Elastic shaft + quadratic load = resonance at k×4.0 boundary.
  This is a real physical instability, not a simulator artifact.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PROPOSED FIX: Discrete Multiplier Options (Menu widget)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  "0.5× (light braking)"     → k = 0.5 × k_nom
  "0.75×"                    → k = 0.75 × k_nom
  "1.0× (design nominal)"    → k = k_nom
  "1.2×"                     → k = 1.2 × k_nom
  "1.5× (optimal — max P)"   → k = 1.5 × k_nom
  "2.5× (over-braked)"       → k = 2.5 × k_nom
  "4.0× ⚠ (instability)"     → k = 4.0 × k_nom

  Benefits:
  - Scales correctly for ANY P_rated (10 kW → 50 kW → 100+ kW)
  - Each choice maps to a validated physics regime
  - Instability boundary visible and warns user
  - Matches v2 sweep grid exactly
