What This Project Proves

Grounded in code, docs, and simulation results — no speculation

Already proved
Structural optimisation
11.47 kg Minimum feasible 10 kW TRPT shaft mass (v5, BEM-coupled, Euler + torsion constraints)
39.30 kg Minimum feasible 50 kW shaft — 51% lighter than fixed-CT models predicted
120/120 islands n_lines = 8 selected unanimously across all DE campaigns (v4 + v5)
−31% mass Constant L/r ring spacing vs uniform spacing — key structural insight
449% mass penalty Adding torsional constraint: 2.81 kg (Euler-only, invalid) → 15.44 kg. Then constant L/r recovers it to 11.47 kg
Torsional physics
Emergent collapse Tensile-only spring law naturally produces torsional collapse — no detection criterion needed. First simulator to model this correctly
Tulloch wave Elastic torsional rebound wave observed in furl simulation — consistent with Tulloch PhD theory
Lift device
8× better Rotary lifter lift stability vs passive kite (3.6% vs 30.1% tension CoV). Hub excursion 3.9× smaller
245 N True lift required for 10 kW system after correcting phantom rotor lift bug — enables accurate lift device sizing
MPPT & control
Flat peak k×1.0–1.2 MPPT gain is near-optimal at nominal calibration. Ramp dynamics are the real control problem
Twist ≠ MPPT signal Shaft twist tracks wind speed but is ambiguous — can't distinguish under- from over-braking at same twist value
Economics
5.10 p/kWh LCOE at 30% CF, 7% discount, 20yr life. Capital cost £11,724; CFRP tubes just £126
2 months Carbon payback period — 1.51 gCO₂e/kWh embodied energy
Still needs proof
Aerodynamics
CFD validation of n=8 Cp BEM strip theory not validated above n=6. Blade-to-blade wake interference, blockage, and high-solidity corrections unmodelled. Cannot adopt n=8 for hardware without this
Free elevation angle β All campaigns fix β=30°. Cold-start analysis suggests optimum near β≈26°. Joint β + structural optimisation not yet run
Structural
Dynamic fatigue All sizing against static peak envelope only. No S-N model, no cyclic 1P/2P loading, no accumulated damage estimate
Ring radial deformation Rings are point masses — inward buckling deformation not modelled. Matters when FoS approaches 1.0
Multi-segment backline Single spring-damper misses catenary sag and forward hub drift. Needed for realistic collapse scenarios
Control & dynamics
Cyclic blade pitch Blade AoA fixed. Bridling as control input discussed but not implemented. Critical for above-rated operation
Centrifugal rotor expansion Dynamic radius expansion under spin not integrated into full-system ODE. AlphaAnemo/SomeAWE have studied this as a scaling pathway
Torsional wave propagation Tulloch wave observed in simulation but not analytically characterised. No model of resonance or damping
Scale & deployment
Network deployment Multi-turbine array interactions, wake effects, and coordinated control not modelled
Launch & retrieval sequence Ramp from ground to operating altitude not simulated. Contact physics missing
